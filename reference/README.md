## Open question

The master brand is dusty rose (`#D49790`) on cream; the GOALS dashboard is deep
forest, sage and terracotta with no rose at all. Confirm whether GOALS is a
sub-brand with its own colourway, or whether rose should carry through as an
accent, before design tokens are committed to code.

Related: the logo tagline reads `PLAN ORGANISE ACHIEVE`, the dashboard sidebar
reads `PLAN · SAVE · ACHIEVE`.
